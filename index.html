Tax report

Hungarian tax is worked out in forints at the MNB rate. This report uses the MNB rate stored when each trade was logged; positions bought before logging started have no forint cost, so their gains show as unknown. A starting point for your tax return. Also worth asking your broker: whether your account can be a TBSZ (long-term investment account), which makes gains tax-free after five years.

  2. Resource owner benipkun, then Only select repositories → market-council-state
